TANZANIA XDR-TB CASE: MUTATION ANALYSIS

1. mmpR5 (Rv0678)
Two frameshift variants were reported:
- Asp47fs: first reported in isolate 3.
- Glu49fs: first reported in isolate 5, reaching 71% frequency in isolate 8.

These findings suggest changes in the bacterial subpopulations over time.

2. gyrA
- Ala90Val: reported at 10% in isolate 1 and not detected in isolate 2.
- Asp94Gly: first reported in isolate 3 and detected in later isolates.

These variants are associated with fluoroquinolone resistance.

3. ald
- A frameshift variant was reported at 17% in isolates 5 and 6.
- It was not detected in isolate 8.

4. Overall interpretation
Serial whole-genome sequencing can help identify changes in resistance-associated variants over time. The findings highlight the importance of monitoring bacterial populations during treatment.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION
These results are based on information reported in the published case study. They are not independently reproduced from the patient's original sequencing reads.
